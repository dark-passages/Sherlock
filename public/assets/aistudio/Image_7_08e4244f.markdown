## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a **Modern Tactile Minimalism**â€”merging the crisp, editorial clarity of daily puzzle publications with the quiet, deliberate craft of classic detective stationery.

### Brand Personality & Audience
- **Personality:** Sharp, observant, calm, and intellectually rewarding. It feels like a well-printed broadsheet puzzle paired with heavy cotton paper and a freshly sharpened pencil.
- **Target Audience:** Daily puzzle enthusiasts, logic game players, and commuters seeking a focused, distraction-free mental ritual.
- **Emotional Response:** Focused calm, tactile satisfaction, and quiet confidence. Every interactionâ€”marking a cell, crossing off a clue, or confirming a deductionâ€”should feel deliberate and crisp.

### Visual Direction
- **Editorial Precision:** Generous breathing room, clear typographic hierarchy, and restrained color usage ensure the logic grid remains the undisputed focal point.
- **Tactile Stationery:** Warm paper-like surfaces, hairline borders, and subtle elevation cues give digital cards and grid cells a physical, grounded presence without resorting to heavy skeuomorphism.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in warm, low-glare paper tones and high-contrast ink, reserving saturated hues strictly for interactive focus and logical state feedback.

### Surface & Ink Hierarchy
- **Canvas Background (`#F8F7F4`):** Warm alabaster/ivory that reduces eye strain during extended deduction sessions.
- **Elevated Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Crisp white for active puzzle cards, clue drawers, and modal dialogs, creating clear separation from the canvas.
- **Hairline Border (`#E5E2DC`):** Subtle warm-gray 1px rules used to define grid cells, card edges, and dividers.
- **Primary Ink (`#1C1B18`):** Deep charcoal for puzzle titles, clue statements, and primary grid labels.
- **Secondary Ink (`#6E6B64`):** Muted slate for category headers, timer readouts, and supporting metadata.

### Accent & Semantic States
- **Primary Accent (`#2B4C7E`):** Detective-indigo/navy used for primary actions, active cell selection rings, and focus indicators.
- **Confirmed / Selected (`#2A7B5B`):** Emerald green indicating verified deductions, completed clues, and positive checkmarks in the logic grid.
- **Negated / Eliminated (`#C93B3B`):** Semantic crimson reserved for crossed-out possibilities, "X" marks, and contradiction warnings.

### Accessibility
All text-on-surface combinations maintain at least a 4.5:1 contrast ratio. Semantic states (emerald and crimson) must always be paired with distinct iconography (checkmarks vs. crosses) so color is never the sole indicator of logical state.

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances the approachable, geometric warmth of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headings with the crisp, utilitarian legibility of **Inter** for clues, grid labels, and UI controls.

### Usage Guidelines
- **Tabular Numbers:** Enable `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` on all timers, move counters, grid coordinates, and puzzle dimensions so numbers align cleanly without horizontal jitter.
- **Clue Readability:** Clue statements use `body-lg` or `body-md` with generous line height to support rapid scanning and cross-referencing.
- **Grid Headers:** Category and item labels along the logic grid axes use `label-md` or `label-sm` in uppercase or sentence case with subtle letter spacing for crisp separation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a **Fixed-Max-Width Centered Grid** philosophy, ensuring the logic matrix and clue list remain comfortably within the user's natural field of vision on large monitors while adapting cleanly to smaller viewports.
